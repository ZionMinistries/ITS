Founder's Word

It is our earnest desire to inspire millions of our young men and women who seek the truth. We welcome them. We covet the prayers of all who love the Lord Jesus Christ and who are interested in the young people of India."

Purpose

ITS seeks:

1.To train men and women based on a strong biblical position in the classroom and/or in their own learning laboratories of ministry and life.

2.To provide a unique opportunity for secular education along with the study of the Bible.

Doctrine

Doctrine is Baptist. However, students of all religions and denominations are admitted if they have a genuine interest in the study of the Bible.

2 years Bible Study along with PU/Class 11 for SSLC/10th passed

3. B.B.S. (Bachelor of Biblical Studies)
3 years of Bible Study along with B.A./B.Com./B.C.A. for PU/Class 12/ Intermediate passed

4. B.B.S. Hons. (Bachelor of Biblical Studies Hons.)
4 years full-time Bible study for SSLC / 10th passed

5. M.B.S. (Master of Biblical Studies)
2-3 Years of full-time Bible Study for those with B.B.S./Equivalent or any degree passed

6. D.B.S. (Doctor of Biblical Studies)
2-3 years of full-time study

Unique Opportunity For
Secular Study

A student doing A.B.S. can join Pre-university class (Arts/Commerce); A B.B.S. student can join B.A./B.Com./B.C.A. in Crossland College, an affiliated College in the same Campus.

Crossland College

Established in 1984, recognized by UGC, affiliated to Mangalore University, Reaccredited by NAAC with B++.

Ranked as the 78th best Arts College in India by 'India Today'.

Campus

ITS is on the thirty acre Priority Highlands Campus. Priority Highlands is situated in Chanthar, Brahmavar, Udupi District, Karnataka State. The same Campus is the home of other institutions - Brahmavar Baptist Church, Little Rock Indian School, and Crossland College.

Important

ITS insists on strict discipline and Christian testimony. Hence only those who have genuine interest in the study of the Bible and can submit to rules and authority fully, only need to apply.

Institutions in the Campus follow dress and appearance code.

Fee; Scholarship

The current Financial Information gives fee details. A few scholarships are available for the needy. Opportunity to work in the Campus to earn money is available. Those who committed to full-time Bible study and ministry may receive Ministry Scholarship to cover the expenses.
